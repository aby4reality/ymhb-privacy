Yoruba Methodist Hymnal (YMHB)

Privacy policy

Effective 7 November 2026 · Applies to the YMHB app for Android and iOS

In short: the app does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

Who we are

The Yoruba Methodist Hymnal app ("the app") is developed by Oluwafemi Ajayi of Remaj IT Solutions & Consultancy, Lagos, Nigeria ("we", "us"). You can contact us at remajits@gmail.com.

Information the app collects

None. The app does not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, location, contacts or any other personal information, and it does not create user accounts.

The hymns are built into the app, so it works fully offline and does not send your searches or reading activity anywhere.

Information stored on your device

To work as you expect, the app keeps a small amount of information only on your device:

  • the hymns you mark as favourites;
  • your settings, such as text size, text alignment, light or dark mode, and which hymn book you last used.

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app's data in your phone's settings or by uninstalling the app. Your device's own backup service (for example Google or iCloud backup) may include it in your personal backups, under that service's terms.

Feedback e-mails

If you choose "Help & feedback" or "Report a mistake", the app opens your own e-mail app with a message addressed to us. Nothing is sent unless you press send. If you do, we receive your e-mail address and whatever you write, and we use it only to reply to you and to correct the hymns. We do not add you to any mailing list or share your message with anyone.

Sharing

When you share a hymn or the app, the app hands the text to your phone's standard share menu, and you choose where it goes. The app itself does not see or keep what you share.

Third parties, advertising and tracking

The app contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting services and no tracking of any kind, and it does not share data with third parties.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how it handles information (for example, by adding optional online features), we will update this page and the effective date above before that version is released.
